Hello, I run this salon.

Six years ago, in 2020, I opened Black Button right here in Jagannath Puri. I grew up watching my mother mix amla, hibiscus and coconut oil for our hair at home, and I never forgot how healthy it left us feeling.

My cousin was the first to say, "Why not bring that care to a salon?" So we did. Today we mix that old wisdom with modern styling, and we choose products that are kind to your hair and skin.

Whether you come in for a quick trim or a full waxing session, you will be treated like family.
